# PowerBI-Sales-Dashboard
Interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard using Power BI
Power BI Sales Dashboard


Project Overview

This project focuses on transforming sales data (excel) into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards, data modeling, and DAX calculations.

The dashboard provides an analytical view of sales performance across different regions, product categories, sales channels, and campaigns in 4 pages (P1:Landing , P2:Sales Trends , P3: Product & supply analysis , P4: Store & OrderStatus).

Key Features

 Sales Performance: Analysis of Net Sales and overall sales performance.
 Geographical Analysis: Visualization of sales distribution across Iranian provinces.
 Product Category Analysis: Comparison of sales performance across product categories.
 Sales Channel Analysis: Comparison of Online and In-store sales.
 Campaign Analysis: Evaluation of sales performance by campaign.
 Interactive Visualizations: Dynamic data exploration using filters, slicers, and bookmarks.

Dashboard Analysis

1. Sales Overview

Analysis of overall sales performance and key business metrics.

2. Geographical Sales Analysis

Visualization and comparison of sales across different provinces using geographical maps.

3. Product Category Analysis

Analysis of product category contributions to total Net Sales, including:

 Mobile & Tablets
 Home & Kitchen

4. Sales Channel Analysis

Comparison of sales performance across two main channels:

 In-store
 Online

Analysis of each channel’s contribution to total Net Sales.

5. Campaign Analysis

Evaluation and comparison of Net Sales across different marketing campaigns.
